ABOUT THE SYSTEM

Player Scouting System

The Player Scouting System is a web-based platform designed to help football academies and scouts manage, evaluate, and discover talented players efficiently.

Academies can add player information, maintain player profiles, and manage scouting data. Scouts can view available players and evaluate their skills and performance.

The system also includes an admin approval system to manage academies and scouts and ensure that only approved users can access the platform.
